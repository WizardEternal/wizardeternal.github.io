Research experience

  • Identifiability in joint spectral-timing analysis of X-ray data

    July 2026 – Present

    Short paper in preparation, TIFR

    Methods collaboration with Prof. Sudip Bhattacharyya at TIFR: two X-ray instruments with complementary energy and time resolution observe the same window, and the question is which timing observables the pair determines and which the data leave free at any exposure. Computing the attainable sets exactly shows which observables no exposure can pin down, and which ones only need more counts.

  • XRISM/Resolve forecast for phase-resolved wind spectroscopy of heartbeat states

    July 2026 – Present

    Manuscript in preparation, sole author, TIFR

    Monte-Carlo forecast of what Resolve at 4.5 eV would recover from the disk wind across the heartbeat cycle of GRS 1915+105 and IGR J17091-3624, on an instrument model tied to two real sources. An equilibrium wind reaches a 3σ detection probability of at most 0.07, so a modulation detected at 3σ is itself evidence of a dynamic wind; the dynamic track is rejected against a constant wind with probability 0.84 to 0.98 at 3σ in 162 ks. A 3σ detection at full amplitude needs 130 to 180 ks net, which brackets the standing 150 ks award.

  • Machine learning and simulation-based inference: when a learned posterior can be trusted

    June 2026 – Present

    Two papers, sole author, Independent

    Two sole-author methods papers on the reliability of amortized neural inference. Three independent misspecification checks are all blind to a 3% detector gain shift that still biases the photon index by +0.02, and marginalizing the gain during training leaves that bias in place; a Gaia XP catalog audit finds its BP-band residual weakly contaminates the selection, at a spurious rate of 0.09±0.02 against a 0.053 baseline.

  • Cross-band (X-ray × optical) periodicity search for SMBHBs

    May 2026 – August 2026

    Published in ApJ, sole author, Independent

    Sole-author search over 1194 Swift-BAT hard X-ray AGN and 175 4XMM-DR14 AGN, against ZTF and ASAS-SN optical. Requiring the same period in both bands makes the joint false-alarm rate the product of the independent single-band rates. No source survives, and the null becomes the first completeness-corrected constraint on the co-periodic fraction: under 3% at hard X-ray fractional modulation above 0.3, over periods of 100 to 900 days. The limiting factor is the X-ray monitoring cadence.

Machine learning and software projects

Supermassive black hole binaries, gravitational waves & multi-messenger

  • TNG SMBHB Population Pipeline

    Turns the IllustrisTNG black hole merger catalog into a multi-messenger detectability funnel across PTA, LISA and EM survey windows; found and fixed a catalog bug that forced q = 1.

  • Stripe 82 SMBHB Periodicity Search

    Searches the MacLeod et al. Stripe 82 quasar catalog with per-object damped-random-walk false-alarm nulls, controlling the red noise that fakes periods in Lomb-Scargle searches: 44 candidates at 99% significance, 10 at 99.9%.

  • LISA Gap Imputer

    Transformer pipeline for imputing gaps in LISA strain from antenna repointing and micrometeorite hits, trained on colored-noise realizations with injected SMBHB chirps and galactic binaries. Benchmarked against spline and Gaussian-process baselines.

  • Post-Newtonian Inspiral Simulator

    Simulates SMBHB inspirals from 105 to 1010 M☉ against NANOGrav and LISA sensitivity curves, with EM detectability for Stripe 82, PTF and LSST.

  • ZTF Kilonova Vetting Pipeline

    Reproduces the human-vetting decisions from the Kasliwal et al. 2020 ZTF O3 gravitational-wave follow-up campaign with a feature-based classifier. It also catalogues where such pipelines fail on sparse real-time photometry.

X-ray & high-energy astrophysics

  • SBI Calibration Benchmark

    Calibration and misspecification tests for amortized neural posterior estimation in X-ray spectral fitting: misspecification detection across three schemes, a gain-marginalized retrain, and an exact nested-sampling cross-check. PyTorch, sbi, ultranest.

  • XRB Luminosity Function Forward Model

    Completeness-folded likelihood and hierarchical stacking for X-ray binary luminosity functions; removes the slope bias of detection-naive fits and sharpens the slope by 2.4×.

  • Galactic X-ray Source Classifier

    Calibrated gradient-boosted classifier (STAR/CV/XRB/AGN) for eROSITA Galactic-plane sources with Gaia counterparts, with leakage-free calibration, a label-circularity audit, and out-of-distribution flagging. Assigns confident labels to 93% of ~20,750 unlabeled inner-plane sources.

  • AutoRXTE: Automated RXTE Analysis Pipeline

    End-to-end RXTE analysis toolkit, from automated HEASARC data acquisition through GTI filtering, event extraction, power spectra, automated XSPEC fitting, and color-color diagrams.

  • Automated AstroSat Data Download Tool

    Built Selenium-based system for batch-downloading observations with filtering by source name, date range, and instrument configuration (website only allows single downloads)
