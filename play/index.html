Toys from my papers

These are interactive toys built from my papers and code. Each one runs in your browser, and each has notes underneath on what it leaves out and where the numbers come from.

Before you start

Each toy has its own How to play box with a goal at the top.

  1. Drag any 3D view to turn it.
  2. The 3D scenes use WebGL, so they work best on a laptop or desktop.
